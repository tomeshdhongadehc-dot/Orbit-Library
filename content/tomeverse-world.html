World rules in this mock-up
  • Stack: the newest task is next. Old tasks sink to the bottom and go stale. Queue: the oldest task is next.
  • Finish: the island grows a tower (up to 6). Drop: the task vanishes, nothing grows.
  • Defer: the next task moves to the other end of the pile.
  • Skip a day: every task ages. Three days old turns red.
  • Pressure = tasks + 2 per stale task. Under 4 clear sky, 4 to 7 clouds, 8 or more storm.
